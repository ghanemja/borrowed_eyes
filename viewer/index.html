Whose eyes

These views are approximations built from published measurements of each animal's color receptors, sharpness, field of view and flicker rate. Figures are rounded and vary between individuals and studies. Nobody can know what an animal experiences; this shows the information its eyes can gather. Bee and fly colors use the standard false-color convention, since ultraviolet has no color of its own on a screen.
